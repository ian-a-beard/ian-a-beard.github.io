My longest hike ever

A photo sequence with abridged commentary of my hike to the top of Pic du Canigou

Preamble

In the year 2024 from October 11 to November 24, I lived on a small horse farm in the foothills of the Pyrenees Mountains in France. On my last full day before leaving I summited the nearby Canigou mountain.

I had been fixing to hike it during my stay, but it wasn't until the last day that both the weather and my confidence aligned and I finally gave it a shot.

The day hike ended up being my longest so far, totaling 28.4 miles (45.7 km). I began early in the morning in order to finish before sundown. In total, it took me 12 hours and 53 minutes to complete. Here is a sequence of photos I took which illustrate the endeavor.
